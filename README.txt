SHREE SAWARIYA SOLAR PROJECT — WEBSITE

Pages included:
- index.html       Home
- about.html       About the business
- services.html    Six solar product/service categories
- subsidy.html     Subsidy information and official portal link
- contact.html     Contact details and WhatsApp enquiry form

How to preview:
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. For hosting, upload the contents of this folder to your website's public_html (or web root) directory.

Customisation notes:
- Business details and services were taken from the supplied poster.
- The supplied poster is included at assets/solar-project-poster.jpg.
- The enquiry form opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message to +91 85039 21958. It does not store submissions on a server.
- Phone, email, address, and official subsidy portal link can be updated in the HTML files.
- Google Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts; a network connection is needed for those fonts. The site otherwise uses local CSS and JavaScript.
- Subsidy wording is intentionally conditional. Verify current eligibility and benefits on the official PM Surya Ghar portal.

This is a static front-end website. No admin panel, database, or server-side form processing is included.
