Frequently asked questions

Solar subsidy: quick answers

Does every solar installation qualify for a subsidy?

No. Eligibility depends on the current scheme rules, customer category, system type, installation requirements and other conditions.

Where can I check the latest subsidy details?

Use the official PM Surya Ghar portal and follow the latest instructions published by the Government of India and relevant authorities.

Can Shree Sawariya Solar Project guide me?

Contact our team to discuss your rooftop solar requirement and understand what information or documents you may need to review. Final eligibility is determined by the scheme authorities.

Is the subsidy amount fixed?

Subsidy amounts and programme conditions may be revised. Please verify the current amount on the official portal rather than relying on old advertisements.
